# Peapod bodies

The pea from [concept C](logo-concepts.md#c--peapod) with something to
stand on. Judge on the [bodies sheet](../previews/v10-peapod-bodies.html);
assets live in [assets/](.).

## A · Pilot (figurative)

Peapod head on a small ink body with green boots. A character with no
face — the pod is the head. Best for empty states, success pages, docs
illustrations. File: [peapod-a-pilot.svg](peapod-a-pilot.svg).

## B · Cloak (abstract)

The pod hovers over a gold vessel. No limbs, no posture — the body is
a shape that carries. Most distinctive; best primary-mark candidate
for a business audience. File: [peapod-b-cloak.svg](peapod-b-cloak.svg).

## C · Runner (figurative)

Leaning pod with legs and speed ticks. Made for loading and progress
states — too energetic for the primary mark, ideal as the in-motion
secondary. File: [peapod-c-runner.svg](peapod-c-runner.svg).

## D · Carrier (abstract)

Tall vessel outline holding the peapod at its heart. Enterprise-calm;
closest to concept D with the pea for warmth. File:
[peapod-d-carrier.svg](peapod-d-carrier.svg).

## E · Stick (figurative)

Peapod head on a universal person pictogram: round-cap strokes, open
arms, striding legs. Most human of the set, readable at any size, and
the easiest to animate. File: [peapod-e-stick.svg](peapod-e-stick.svg).

## F · Big head (figurative)

Pod head scaled up, body shrunk beneath it — classic mascot ratio,
short limbs. The body stops competing with the pea. File:
[peapod-f-bighead.svg](peapod-f-bighead.svg).

## G · Slim (figurative)

Thinner strokes, smaller head, longer legs — airport-pictogram ratio.
Calmest human option, best for enterprise surfaces. File:
[peapod-g-slim.svg](peapod-g-slim.svg).

## H · Stride (F mid-walk)

Head tipped, legs split, arms swinging opposite — nothing mirrors
anything. The everyday-workhorse pose. File:
[peapod-h-stride.svg](peapod-h-stride.svg).

## I · Wave (F saying hello)

One arm up with gold motion ticks, head tipped toward it. The
greeter for onboarding, empty states, success screens. File:
[peapod-i-wave.svg](peapod-i-wave.svg).

## J · Dash (F at full tilt)

Leaning forward with gold speed lines — the deploy-is-running pose.
For loading states and launch moments, never the static mark. File:
[peapod-j-dash.svg](peapod-j-dash.svg).

## Recommendation

B as the mark, C as its in-motion companion, A for illustrations. That
trio covers identity, motion, and storytelling from one pea.
