peapod bodies

The pea from concept C, given something to stand on. Eight figurative, two abstract. Pick a letter.

A · Pilot — figurative, standing

The peapod as a head on a small ink body with green boots. Reads as a character without needing a face — the pod itself is the head. Friendliest of the four; the obvious pick for empty states, success pages, and docs illustrations.

B · Cloak — abstract, floating

The pod hovers over a gold vessel. No limbs, no posture — the body is a shape that carries. Most distinctive and most premium; the one that feels designed rather than drawn. Best as the primary mark if business users are the audience.

C · Runner — figurative, in motion

The pod leans forward with legs and speed ticks. Deployments on their way — made for loading states, progress screens, and the CLI. Too energetic for the primary mark; perfect as the secondary, in-motion version of whichever wins.

D · Carrier — abstract, held

A tall vessel outline holding the peapod at its heart. The enterprise-calm option: deployment as container, pod as the living thing inside. Closest to concept D, with the pea for warmth.

E · Stick — figurative, pictogram

The peapod as the head of a universal person symbol. Round-cap strokes, arms open — the most human of the five, readable at any size, and the easiest to animate (wave, walk, point). Best if the brand should feel like it is made of people.

F · Big head — figurative, mascot proportions

The pod head scaled up, the body shrunk beneath it. Large head, short limbs — the classic mascot ratio. Friendliest read of the set; the body stops competing with the pea.

G · Slim — figurative, pictogram proportions

Same stick figure, thinner strokes and a smaller head. The elegant-signage ratio — closer to an airport pictogram than a mascot. Calmest human option; pairs best with enterprise surfaces.

H · Stride — F mid-walk

Caught mid-step: head tipped, legs split, arms swinging opposite. Nothing mirrors anything. The everyday-workhorse pose — walking toward the next deployment.

I · Wave — F saying hello

One arm up with gold motion ticks, head tipped toward it. The greeter — onboarding, empty states, success screens. Asymmetry comes free with the raised arm.

J · Dash — F at full tilt

Whole body leaning forward, gold speed lines behind. Maximum motion — the deploy-is-running pose. Use sparingly: loading states and launch moments, never the static mark.
