Four concepts, zero faces. Each shown on light and dark. Pick a letter.

A · Single seed — closest to current

One dot, tucked bottom-right, off-center so it can never be eyes. The seed sits where a deployment rolls out. Smallest change from the v1 mark; same silhouette, face gone.

Construction: 64-grid, pod rx=18, seed r=8 at (41,41).

B · Pod cluster — the scale-out story

Three pods ascending. No dots at all, so no face is possible. Tells the roadmap truth directly: deployments are long-lived, pods multiply beneath them.

Construction: three rounded squares, 18 / 22 / 22, ascending left to right. Monochrome — survives fax, favicon, embroidery.

gongsho

C · Peapod — most ownable

A literal pod with three seeds in a row. Three dots read as peas, never eyes, because they sit in a horizontal pod. Warmest and most memorable of the four — the one a business user sketches from memory.

Construction: capsule 56×24, rx=12, three seeds r=5.5. Wide shape loves horizontal lockups, needs a stacked variant for square avatars.

gongsho

D · Nested — most enterprise

A deployment shell with the pod at its core. All right angles and one core square — the calmest mark, and the best-dressed for a business audience. Nothing remotely facial.

Construction: shell 48×48, rx=14, 6-unit green stroke; core 16×16 ink square. Redraws cleanly at 16px favicon size.
