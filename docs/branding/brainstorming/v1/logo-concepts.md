# Logo concepts — round 2

The v1 pod mark reads as a face: two dots side by side in a round
shape will always be eyes. These four concepts keep the pod story
with zero eye-pairs. Judge them on the
[concepts sheet](../previews/v9-logo-concepts.html); assets live in
[assets/](.).

## A · Single seed

One dot, tucked bottom-right and off-center — a seed where the
deployment rolls out, never eyes. Closest to the v1 mark: same
silhouette, face gone. File:
[concept-a-single-seed.svg](concept-a-single-seed.svg).

## B · Pod cluster

Three rounded pods ascending, no dots at all. Tells the roadmap truth
directly: deployments are long-lived, pods multiply beneath them.
Monochrome, survives any reproduction. File:
[concept-b-cluster.svg](concept-b-cluster.svg).

## C · Peapod

A literal horizontal pod with three seeds in a row. Three dots read as
peas, never eyes, because of the capsule they sit in. Warmest and most
memorable; needs a stacked variant for square avatars. File:
[concept-c-peapod.svg](concept-c-peapod.svg).

## D · Nested

A deployment shell (green outline) with the pod as a solid core. The
calmest mark and the best-dressed for a business audience; redraws
cleanly at favicon size. File:
[concept-d-nested.svg](concept-d-nested.svg).

## Recommendation

C for memorability with business users, D if the room skews
enterprise-conservative, A if you want the smallest break from v1.
Whichever wins replaces the mark in [Logo](logo.md).
