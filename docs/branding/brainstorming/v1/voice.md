# Voice

Friendly about pods, boring about promises. Warm nouns, plain verbs,
checkable claims.

## Principles

1. Name the concrete thing: files, ports, checks, directories.
2. Promise only what the harness verifies.
3. Let the pods carry the play — round words for containers, flat
   words for guarantees.

## Examples

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| Seamless magical deploys! | Scaffolds, builds, and health-checks each deployment through Caddy. |
| Your apps, supercharged | One Caddy on `:8181` routes every deployment by path prefix. |
| Something went wrong | Deploy failed at `build`: exact error plus completed work, below. |
| Our revolutionary pod synergy | Pods are runtime instances; the deployment outlives scale-out. |
| Stay tuned for awesomeness | Console is next; templates are green now. |

## Tone by surface

- CLI and errors: shortest form. Attempt, exact error, completed work.
  No jokes in the failure path.
- Docs: plain second person, present tense, numbered steps.
- Announcements: one playful sentence allowed up front, then the
  concrete list. Never two in a row.
