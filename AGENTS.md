# AGENTS.md

## Commits

Use Conventional Commits: `<type>(<scope>): <subject>`.

- `type`: `feat`, `fix`, `docs`, `chore`, `refactor`, `test`, `ci`,
  `build`, `perf`, `style`, or `revert`.
- `scope`: optional; name the area touched, e.g. `react-ui`, `tools`,
  `harness`.
- `subject`: lowercase, imperative, no trailing period. Say what the
  change does.
- Body: optional, wrapped near 72 columns, for detail and rationale.
- Breaking changes: footer of `BREAKING CHANGE: <migration detail>`.
